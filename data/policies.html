Shop policies

Questions about an order? Email me at edmcveigh.art@gmail.com.

SHIPPING

Shipping is free on all US orders. I currently ship to US customers only. If you're outside the US and really want something, email me and we'll see about arranging shipping.

Payment in full is due before anything ships.

Stickers ship by First Class letter mail, without tracking. Please allow 2–4 business days for me to process your order, plus 2–5 days in the mail. If your stickers haven't arrived after 10 days, email me and I'll help.

Original paintings ship within 2–3 business days by USPS Ground Advantage, with tracking. Every painting is fully insured. If it's damaged in shipping, I'll make it right: a new frame, or a full refund if the painting is damaged beyond repair. Please report damage promptly, no later than 30 days after delivery.

House portraits ship by USPS Ground Advantage once the final payment is in, with the same full insurance as paintings. Local pickup or delivery in the Philly area is also available. Email me to arrange it.

RETURNS

Stickers: all sales are final. But if I made a mistake, like sending the wrong stickers, or they arrived damaged or never arrived, email me and I'll make it right with a replacement or a refund.

Original paintings: you can return a painting within 30 days of delivery. You pay return shipping, and I'll refund you once it arrives back in its original condition.

House portraits: because each one is custom, portraits can't be returned.

HOUSE PORTRAIT COMMISSIONS

  1. Deposit. You pay 50% at checkout. It covers materials and my time to draw the pencil sketch. Ordered by mistake or changed your mind? Email me within 2 days of ordering, and if I haven't started yet, I'll refund the deposit in full. Once I've started, the deposit is non-refundable.
  2. Sketch. I send you the pencil sketch. If you'd like small changes, ask now. This is the only stage where changes are possible.
  3. Your choice. If you decide not to continue, you owe nothing more and the commission ends there.
  4. Painting. If you continue, I finish the painting. The remaining 50% is due when it's done, and I'll email you an invoice. The portrait ships or is handed off after that payment.

Turnaround is usually 2 to 4 weeks from sketch approval, depending on my schedule. If you have a firm deadline, like a closing gift, email me before ordering and I'll do my best to make it work.
