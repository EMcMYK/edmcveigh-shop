Privacy

This is a small shop run by one person. I only collect what I need to make, ship and support your order, and I never sell or rent your information.

When you order

Checkout happens on Stripe’s secure payment page. Stripe collects your name, email, shipping address and payment details. Your card details go straight to Stripe and never reach me or this website.

I receive your name, email, shipping address and what you ordered, so I can ship it and send you updates. For house portraits, Stripe also keeps your details on file so the final invoice can be sent to you, along with any deadline or notes you add at checkout.

Your address is used to buy a shipping label and is passed to the shipping carrier (usually USPS) to deliver your order. Sales tax, when it applies, is calculated by Stripe from your address.

When you contact me

The contact form sends your name, email and message to my inbox through a form service called Web3Forms. If you email me directly, including photos of your home for a portrait, I keep those emails to answer you and finish your order.

When you browse

There are no ads or tracking pixels on this site. I count visits with a simple counter built into this site and edmcveigh.com. It doesn’t use cookies and doesn’t store your IP address or anything that identifies you. It records which pages are viewed, the site or link you came from (for example, Pinterest), your country, and whether you’re on a phone or computer, so I can see what’s working. Your cart and your light or dark mode choice are saved in your own browser, not sent to me.

The site is hosted by Cloudflare and its fonts come from Google Fonts. Like any website, they receive basic technical information such as your IP address in order to deliver pages and keep the site secure.

Sharing

I don’t sell, rent or trade your information, and I don’t share it with anyone except the services above, and only what they need to take payment, deliver your order or deliver your message.

Your information

I keep order records as long as I need them for your order and for tax records. To see, correct or delete what I have about you, email me at hello@edmcveigh.com.
